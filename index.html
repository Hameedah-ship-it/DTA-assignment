Why Choose DTA?


At Digital Training Academy, we are committed to empowering individuals with the skills they need to thrive in today's digital economy. Our comprehensive curriculum, expert instructors, and hands-on training ensure that you gain the knowledge and experience necessary to succeed.

32,000 Nigerians to Be Empowered with Free World-Class Training

The Federal Government of Nigeria, in partnership withCoursera, is offering 32,000 free learning licenses to empower citizens with in-demand digital and professional skills.

This initiative is part of the National Skilling Project, designed to bridge the skills gap, improve employability, and open new opportunities for Nigerians in today's competitive job market.

The government launched this program to ensure every Nigerian, regardless of background or resources, can participate in the digital economy.

Beyond providing free online learning, training centers have been set up across the country, equipped with internet access and learning devices, so even those without personal gadgets can fully participate and benefit from the program.
